Research Experience

Yale University

Aug 2023 – Present

Graduate Research Assistant

Advisor: Prof. Lu Lu

New Haven, CT

  • One-shot neural operator learning

    Proposed a data-efficient method for PDE solution operators that learns from only one data pair by leveraging the locality of derivatives, enabling generalization to varied new inputs and geometries.

  • Symmetry-constrained scientific law discovery

    Developed a multi-agent reinforcement learning framework with structure-aware LSTMs and physical symmetry constraints to discover interpretable constitutive laws in crystal plasticity across 120 crystal orientations and 12 interacting slip systems. Identified nonlinear pairwise coupling beyond conventional additive hardening, reducing shear-rate relative L2 error from 18.1% to 9.7%.

  • Physics-informed neural operators on unknown manifolds

    Developed physics-informed DeepONet for PDEs on unknown manifolds, using RBF kernels and diffusion maps to approximate differential operators. Integrated the method into a Bayesian MCMC framework to estimate diffusion coefficients from sparse, noisy point-cloud observations.

  • Physics-informed ML for chromatin inference

    Developed a parallel physics-informed neural network using mixed formulation and domain decomposition to infer spatially varying biophysical parameters (methylation rates and chromatin–lamina affinity) from single-cell STORM microscopy data, demonstrating robust recovery under up to 5% observational noise in synthetic benchmarks.

  • Time-adaptive neural operator learning

    Developed TANTE, a time-adaptive Transformer-based operator-learning framework using neural Taylor expansion for continuous-time PDE prediction, achieving 60–80% performance improvements and 30–40% inference speedups over baselines.
